# Confidential Contract Explainer

## What This Is

Confidential Contract Explainer is an internal AI assistant that helps you understand your contracts, agreements, and other legal or business documents faster. Upload a document, ask questions in plain language, and get answers that point directly to the specific section or clause they came from.

This tool was built specifically so that confidential company documents can be reviewed with AI assistance without that content ever being sent to an external, third-party service. Everything runs on internal infrastructure only.

## Why This Exists

Reading through long contracts to find a specific clause, whether it's a liability cap, a termination condition, or a renewal trigger, takes time. Public AI chat tools can help, but using them means pasting potentially confidential contract text into a service outside company control. This tool was created to close that gap: giving employees the speed and convenience of an AI assistant, without the confidentiality risk.

## Who This Is For

Anyone at the company who regularly works with contracts, agreements, policies, or other dense documents and needs quick, reliable answers about their contents. No technical background is required to use it.

## What It Can Do

- Upload a contract or similar document and ask natural-language questions about its contents.
- Get answers that cite the exact section or clause they were derived from, so you can verify them yourself.
- Have a multi-turn conversation about a document, asking follow-up questions naturally.
- Switch between multiple documents you've previously uploaded.

## What It Cannot Do

- Provide legal advice or a substitute for legal review.
- Guarantee answers are complete, final, or binding for high-stakes decisions.
- Process scanned image-only documents.
- Compare multiple contracts simultaneously.
- Draft new contract language.

## How to Use It

1. Upload a document (PDF or Word format).
2. Ask questions in the chat window.
3. Review the cited section/clause alongside each answer.
4. Ask follow-up questions as needed within the same session.

## Data & Confidentiality

- Documents you upload are only accessible to you and are not shared with other users by default.
- Nothing you upload or ask is sent outside internal company systems.
- You can request deletion of any document you've uploaded at any time.

## Status

> This tool is currently in an **internal pilot phase**. Feedback is welcome and will directly shape future improvements.

If you notice an answer that seems incorrect or misleading, please report it so it can be reviewed.
